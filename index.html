Privacy Policy — Health Tracker

Publisher: Pratham Mishra • Effective Date: November 19, 2026

Health Tracker is built with an offline-first architecture. All daily step counts, hydration logs, body measurements, and reminder schedules are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to external servers.

Permissions Used

  • Physical Activity: Used solely on-device to read your phone's hardware step counter.
  • Notifications & Alarms: Used to deliver local Walk and Drink Water reminders.
  • Internet: Used only when you choose to initiate a one-time Pro upgrade checkout.

In-App Payments

Optional one-time Pro upgrades are processed securely through Razorpay. Health Tracker does not collect, view, or store your UPI ID, card details, or banking credentials.

Health & Fitness Disclaimer

BMI, BMR, and TDEE calculations provided by Health Tracker are general wellness estimates and do not constitute medical advice.
